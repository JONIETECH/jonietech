About Alur Hymns

Our Mission

Alur Hymns was created with a mission to preserve and make accessible the rich tradition of Alur worship songs. We believe in connecting people with their spiritual heritage through technology.

This app is designed to serve the Alur-speaking community in Westnile Uganda (areas of Nebbi, Paidha, Packwach etc) and even those in diaspora, providing a digital platform for worship in their native language. Whether you're at church, at home, or on the go, Alur Hymns ensures you have access to these timeless spiritual songs.

Our team is dedicated to continually improving the app, adding new features, and ensuring the content is accurate and complete. We welcome feedback from our users to help us make Alur Hymns the best worship companion possible.

Thank you for supporting Alur Hymns and helping us preserve this important cultural and spiritual heritage.
